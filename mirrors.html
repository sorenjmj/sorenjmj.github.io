Removable mirror mounts

UBC Baja chassis team, 2025–26

Goal

Give drivers a wider view around the car using small 1.25 in convex blind-spot mirrors on the front roll cage.

Design

Each mount is a 3D-printed PETG dovetail clamp that fits a roll cage tube, so the mirror can be removed. A heat-set insert and a set screw stop the mirror from moving.
