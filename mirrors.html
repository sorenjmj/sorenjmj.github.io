Removable mirror mounts

UBC Baja chassis team, designed May 2026

3D-printed clamp-on mounts that hold convex blind-spot mirrors on the car’s front roll cage, so the driver can see the wheels, the area around the car, and traffic approaching from behind.

Requirements

The Baja SAE rulebook has no requirements for mirrors, so I set my own from what the driver needs:

  • Show the car’s wheels and the area around the car.
  • Give enough rear view to spot a passing car.
  • Block as little of the driver’s forward view as possible.
  • Hold firmly on the tube without shifting under the car’s normal vibration, while still being removable.

Design decisions

  • Convex mirrors, 1.25 in diameter: a convex surface gives the widest field of view for a given mirror size, and standard 1.25 in truck blind-spot mirrors met that need off the shelf.
  • Front roll cage mounting: mounted here, each mirror can be angled for the best view. Placement balances the rear field of view against how much the mirror blocks the view ahead.
  • Dovetail clamp: two 3D-printed halves lock together around the tube with a dovetail joint, so the mount needs no permanent attachment to the frame and the mirror comes off easily.
  • Set screw and heat-set insert: a set screw in the back of the mount stops it from moving, threaded into a heat-set insert pressed into the printed part.
  • PETG: I printed the mounts in PETG for its strength.

Fit testing

The hardest requirement was the fit. The clamp had to hold on the tube without moving under normal vibration, yet still come off. A printed part’s dimensions depend on the printer and material, so I couldn’t get this from CAD alone. I printed a few iterations with different tolerances and tested each one on the tube until I found a fit that stayed put and could still be removed.

Build

  1. Print both halves of the mount in PETG.
  2. Press the heat-set insert into the hole in the back, where the set screw goes.
  3. Attach the mirror to the front of the mount.
  4. Slide the two halves together over the front roll cage member.

Parts: PETG filament, 1.25 in blind-spot mirrors, heat-set inserts, set screws.
