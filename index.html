About

I’m a second-year Integrated Engineering student at the University of British Columbia, specializing in mechanical engineering. I joined the UBC Baja SAE team in September 2025 and work in the chassis group, designing, 3D printing, and fabricating parts for an off-road race car.

My work on the team includes a lumbar support insert developed through testing with the team’s drivers, a rule-compliant transponder mount, and removable mirror mounts. I’m now developing composite body panels. Much of this work is shaped by practical constraints: Baja SAE rules, a small testing budget, and parts that must be built, fitted to the car, and used by drivers. A course project paired a sheet-metal claw with an Arduino-compatible controller and an ultrasonic sensor to build an autonomous retrieval robot.

I work in SolidWorks and Fusion 360, hold the CSWA certification, and have experience with waterjet cutting, 3D printing, and chassis fabrication, including frame jigging and tube coping and bending.

I’ve also worked as an overnight camp counselor and volunteered over 250 hours with youth summer camps. Outside school, I’m adventurous and outdoorsy: I play ultimate frisbee, volleyball, and hockey, and I swim and ski.

I’m looking for a 4, 8, or 12-month mechanical engineering co-op starting in May 2027.
