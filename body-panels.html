Composite body panels

UBC Baja, September 2026 to present

Composite cowl and side panels for the UBC Baja car, being developed to give full cockpit debris protection at a targeted 40% weight reduction.

In progress. This project is in the research stage. The goals below are targets, not results.

Goals

  • Design cowl and side panels that meet Baja SAE Rule B.8.5 for full cockpit debris protection, with gaps under 0.25 in.
  • Use CNC-routed multi-part molds and a carbon fibre and fibreglass vacuum-assisted wet layup.
  • Target a 40% weight reduction while keeping rigidity and impact resistance.
  • Add mounting tabs with plastic quick-disconnect fasteners so panels come off without tools during inspection and pit stops.
