Autonomous retrieval robot

Course project, January to February 2026

The retrieval robot uses a sheet-metal claw that closes automatically around an object once an ultrasonic sensor detects it in range. A single servo motor actuates the claw, and an Arduino-compatible board provides control.

Design

The claw consists of two hinged aluminum sheet-metal panels joined at a central plate. Each panel carries three curved finger strips that wrap around the object being held. A servo motor mounted on the plate opens and closes the panels, while an ultrasonic sensor on the plate faces the object. Hinges and small mounting brackets complete the assembly.

Control

An Arduino-compatible board reads the ultrasonic sensor and commands the servo to close the claw when an object enters the grasping range. The sensor was calibrated so that the claw closes at the correct moment.

Performance

The heaviest object held by the claw was a metal baseball bat, approximately 0.8 to 0.9 kg.
