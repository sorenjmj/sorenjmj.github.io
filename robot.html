Autonomous retrieval robot

Course project, January to February 2026

Overview

I designed and built an autonomous robot from aluminum sheet metal and custom mechanical components to retrieve target payloads.

Control

An Arduino-compatible microcontroller drives servo motors using ultrasonic sensor feedback, giving closed-loop control of the mechanism.

Teamwork

In a multi-team showcase, I helped coordinate role selection and design changes under tight testing timelines.
