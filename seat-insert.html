Driver seat lumbar insert

UBC Baja chassis team, 2025–26

Problem

The driver seat did not support the lumbar area well. In the car’s awkward seated position, drivers’ rounded backs hurt.

Constraints

The insert had to sit under the existing seat foam to stay within the Baja SAE foam thickness rule, and the team’s budget for testing was small.

Process

I researched insert options and materials, then 3D printed prototype inserts in PETG and had drivers try them. With a small budget, driver feedback was my main test.

I fitted the shape to the seat CAD and bulged it out around the lumbar area, so it is shallower in the center and thicker toward the edges.

Result

Drivers felt more supported during races, and the back pain in the awkward seated position went away. The car passed technical inspection and competed.
