Transponder mount and cage

UBC Baja chassis team, designed October 2025

A rule-compliant mount that holds the team’s TR2 MX timing transponder vertically on the frame and shields it from debris without blocking its signal.

Design decisions

  • Frame mount: welding a tab to the frame was chosen over a body panel mount for a more consistently vertical orientation.
  • Steel tab: a 0.063 in steel tab welded to the frame carries the transponder, as the rules suggest (B.13.2.3.3).
  • ABS cage: metal and carbon fibre can block the signal (B.13.2.3.4), so the cage is 3D-printed ABS. It encloses every side except the bottom, which must stay open (B.13.2.3.1), and slides off a fixed back piece held by two cotter pins for quick access.

Verification

A jig and a level confirmed the tab was vertical, and the car passed technical inspection with the mount fitted.
