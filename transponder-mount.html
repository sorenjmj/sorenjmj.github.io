Transponder mount and cage

UBC Baja chassis team, 2025–26

Requirement

The Baja SAE rules specify how the timing transponder must be mounted, including a vertical orientation and a clear signal path.

Design decisions

  • I compared mounting to a body panel with mounting to the frame, and chose the frame for a more consistent vertical orientation.
  • The mount is a 0.063 in steel tab welded to the frame.
  • The protective cage is two 3D-printed ABS pieces with cotter pins for tool-free access. I chose ABS because metal and carbon fibre can interfere with the transponder’s signal.

Build

I used a jig and a level to hold the tab vertical while it was fabricated. The mount went through technical inspection on the car.
